About

Monica Everett is the heroine of Secrets of the Silent Witch. In the Kingdom of Ridill, the Seven Sages are the most powerful mages in the land, and Monica earned her place among them while still a teenager. Her title, the Silent Witch, comes from her unique talent: she is the only person known to cast spells without chanting an incantation.

The legend and the girl could hardly be more different. Monica is painfully shy and struggles to speak with other people at all. Rather than enjoy the prestige of a Sage, she lives a reclusive life in a cabin in the mountains, happiest when she is alone with her calculations.

The greatest mage of her generation — who would rather do almost anything than talk to someone.

Unchanted Magic

In this world, magic is built from formulas that a mage normally recites aloud as a chant. Monica developed unchanted magic to spare herself the ordeal of speaking in front of others: she constructs the spell's formula entirely in her head.

Because she needs no chant, her spells are fast, precise and hard to anticipate — and her deep love of mathematics is exactly what makes it possible. When she is overwhelmed, she steadies herself by counting and working through numbers.

The Mission

Monica's quiet life ends when fellow Sage Louis Miller, the Barrier Mage, hands her a secret assignment: protect the kingdom's Second Prince, Felix Arc Ridill, without anyone — the prince included — knowing she is there.

To do it, she enrolls at the elite Serendia Academy under the name Monica Norton, posing as an ordinary student. For someone who fears conversation, a school full of nobles, social rules and schemes is its own kind of battlefield. Keeping her secret while guarding the prince is the heart of the story.

The Series

Secrets of the Silent Witch (Silent Witch: Chinmoku no Majo no Kakushigoto) began as a web novel by Matsuri Isora and was published as a light novel series with illustrations by Nanaka Fujimi. It has been adapted into manga and, in 2025, a television anime.

If you enjoy stories about a hidden genius navigating everyday life, Monica's story — part school drama, part magical mystery — is well worth a look.
